How this works and what it does not show

Pipeline. Sionna ray tracing models a substation at 13 GHz. A 400 MHz OFDM signal reflects off a small drone (radar cross-section 0.01 m²). Static clutter is removed, a range–Doppler map is formed, a CFAR detector finds targets, a 16×8 panel estimates the angle, and a Kalman tracker follows the drone. A governed Llama 3.1 8B agent proposes actions; a policy engine enforces rules; a human approves anything that touches the physical world; every step goes into a hash-chained audit log.

Limits. The drone is a point scatterer; antenna gain is flat; angle estimation is modelled at the detected cell, not a full array simulation; a hovering drone would be invisible to the clutter removal; at the closest approach the drone moves sideways and falls into the Doppler blind zone, so the track coasts for about two frames. The recorded operator is scripted. Agent replies are cached from one real Llama run, so this page needs no GPU.
